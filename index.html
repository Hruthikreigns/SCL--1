About SCL

More than a tournamentA family

The Santhipuram Champions League is the first season of a home-ground cricket league run by Superkings Pavilion Bangalore. Six tournament days, 10-over matches, 15-member squads picked at a live player auction, and a prize table that reaches every team that turns up. Play hard, compete strong, respect everyone, celebrate together.
